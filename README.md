# Awesome-Digital-Experience-Platform

# Top Digital Experience Platform (DXP) Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Enterprise CMS, Personalization, Omnichannel Experience, Content & Customer Journey Orchestration*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Experience Platforms (DXP)**. These systems combine content management, personalization, commerce hooks, and analytics so organizations can deliver consistent experiences across web, mobile, and other channels.

**Examples** include Sitecore, Optimizely DXP, Acquia DXP, Adobe Experience Manager, Liferay, Bloomreach Experience, Progress Sitefinity, Kentico Xperience, CoreMedia, and Magnolia CMS (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: DXP has strong open roots. **Drupal**, **Liferay** (open core), **Magnolia**, **dotCMS**, and headless CMS stacks power many enterprise experiences. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Drupal](https://github.com/drupal/drupal)**  
  Leading open-source CMS/DXP foundation—flexible content architecture, multilingual, and massive module ecosystem; powering Acquia and many enterprise sites.

- **[Liferay Portal (open source)](https://github.com/liferay/liferay-portal)**  
  Open-source digital experience and portal platform—same core lineage as Liferay DXP commercial offerings.

- **[Magnolia CMS (Community)](https://github.com/magnolia-cms)**  
  Open-core Java CMS with strong enterprise DXP capabilities in community and commercial editions.

- **[dotCMS](https://github.com/dotCMS/core)**  
  Open-source hybrid CMS/DXP—content APIs, personalization hooks, and enterprise features.

- **[WordPress + enterprise stacks](https://github.com/WordPress/WordPress)**  
  Ubiquitous open CMS extended with headless frontends and enterprise plugins for experience delivery.

- **[Strapi, Directus, Payload](https://github.com/strapi/strapi)**  
  Open headless CMS platforms used as the content core of composable DXP architectures.

- **[Keycloak + open identity for experiences](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak)**  
  Open identity and access management frequently paired with DXP for customer and partner portals.

- **[Matomo + open analytics](https://github.com/matomo-org/matomo)**  
  Open analytics integrated into experience platforms for privacy-conscious measurement.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Full DXP-style CMS**: Drupal or Liferay open core.
- **Hybrid/Java**: Magnolia community or dotCMS.
- **Composable**: Headless CMS (Strapi/Directus) + Next.js/Nuxt + open CDP/analytics.
- **Composable stacks**: Open CMS + search (OpenSearch) + personalization rules + CDN.
- Commercial DXPs still lead in packaged personalization, marketing automation glue, and vendor SLAs.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**Drupal** or **Liferay** for integrated experience platforms; **headless open CMS** for composable architectures.  
Commercial DXPs (AEM, Sitecore, Optimizely, Acquia, etc.) add enterprise support and marketing-cloud depth.  
Many organizations run Drupal or Liferay successfully at scale; others prefer commercial DXP for support and accelerators. Fully open digital experience stacks are production-proven worldwide.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Experience platforms process personal data for personalization. Comply with privacy law, consent, and accessibility requirements (e.g. WCAG). Secure CMS instances aggressively—they are high-value attack targets.
- Open-source platforms offer flexibility and no license lock-in but require skilled teams. Commercial DXPs shift product roadmap and support to the vendor. Neither replaces clear content governance and performance budgets.
